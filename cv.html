Structural design capability

Each product family has its own design engine with these codes programmed in, so every configuration is checked before a memo, drawing or bill of materials is released.

RC pipes

Codes: ASTM C76M, C497; AASHTO LRFD 2024 §12.10; ACPA; MOMRA.

Loads: Earth (vertical arching), fluid, HL-93 and MOMRA vehicles, dry and submerged.

Analysis: Indirect design (installation Types 1-4); direct Heger design on a closed ring; Eriksson Pipe and SAP2000 cross-checks.

Checks: Flexure, crack control, diagonal tension, radial tension, steel limits, stirrups, flotation.

Jacking pipes

Codes: BS EN 1916, BS 5911-1, DWA-A 161, ATV-DVWK-A 127, EN 1992-1-1.

Loads: Earth by soil group, groundwater, traffic, jacking force with joint deflection.

Analysis: DWA-A 161 ring; packer stiffness; SAP2000 ring frame and 3D solid end-zone model.

Checks: Ring flexure and crack width, fatigue, end-zone splitting and padding ties, collar and packer.

Manholes

Codes: AASHTO LRFD 10th, ASTM C478M, ACI 318M-25, ACI 350, MOMRA live load.

Loads: Earth (ACPA arching), at-rest pressure, HL-93 with surcharge, groundwater and buoyancy, handling.

Analysis: SAP2000 shell model per piece on compression-only joints and springs; Wood-Armer slab design.

Checks: Biaxial flexure, hoop forces, shear, strut-and-tie and punching at openings, cracking, flotation, bearing.

Box culverts

Codes: AASHTO LRFD 10th or MOMRA, ASTM C1433M / C1577, ACI 318M-25.

Loads: Earth (Marston and trench factors), lateral earth, surcharge, water, HL-93, HS20, MOMRA vehicles.

Analysis: SAP2000 frame on compression-only Winkler springs; every vehicle placement evaluated.

Checks: Flexure with thrust, shear, crack control, minimum steel, wall stability, bearing, flotation, lifting.

Steel moulds

Codes: BS EN 10025 (S275JR), EN 1993-1-8, BS EN ISO 3834-3, ISO 5817, BS EN 1916 tolerances.

Loads: Fresh-concrete pressure as a full liquid head with a factor of 1.5 for vibration; lifting.

Analysis: Part-by-part checks inside generated Revit mould sets.

Checks: Lifting lugs, governing ratio per part, product and mould tolerances, shop checks before the first cast.
