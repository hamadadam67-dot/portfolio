Approvals, submittals and technical documents

Memos, catalogues, drawings and prequalification packages prepared for technical review

Consultants approve a precast supplier on paper first: prequalification, material submittals, calculation memos, drawings and test plans, each in the client's format and each needing current certificates.

How it works

Memos, catalogues and drawings come out of the same engines, so a submittal package is assembled from current, checked documents and current certificates.

What I built

  • Calculation memos generated by the design engines, every check traced to its source: 156 RC pipe memos (76 direct, 80 indirect) and full manhole and box culvert memos with SAP2000 read-back.
  • Technical catalogues for the RC pipe range: an 86-page engineering catalogue and a 35-page bilingual public catalogue.
  • A prequalification and material-submittal system: an 18-category reference library, a certificate-expiry register, compliance sheets per product and a folder per client request.
  • The master prequalification package rebuilt in the company identity: 452 pages, 156 bookmarks and a section file per chapter.
  • Inspection and test plans, including three-edge-bearing proof testing to ASTM C497.

Tender analysis and pricing

~4,000 quotations since 2023 across Saudi giga- and mega-projects

Client BOQs arrive in Arabic and English with mixed conventions, and every line has to become a product the factory can design, price, make and supply.

How it works

Each BOQ line is resolved to a product the factory makes and to its SAP material code, then designed where needed, taken off, costed and quoted.

What I built

  • BOQ and item analysis, product design where needed, quantity take-off, costing and drawings for every quotation; SAP material creation since Go-Live.
  • A gated take-off pipeline that resolves BOQ lines to SAP material codes and turns every error found into a reusable check.
  • TABS activity-based costing used with MAS, and a pricing engine with floor, target and max per family.
  • Prequalification and material submittals backed by an 18-category reference library and a certificate-expiry register.
